## I. What this inventory reveals that the summary hid

1. **The estate is running systems most companies do not have, on free hardware.** A **judge-drift
   detector** (`eval/`), an **agent circuit breaker** (`telemetry/`), a **real dead-letter queue**
   (`messaging/`), **per-lane GitHub App identities** (`github-app/lanes.json`), **51 generated
   namespace fences**, **five branch-protection rulesets as JSON**, **per-tenant virtual clusters**
   (`sandbox/vcluster`), and a **control-loop registry** (`config/control_loops.toml`).

2. **EU AI Act Article 9 risk management, adopted voluntarily** (`platform/ai/`) — for *every*
   system, regardless of obligation. That is a compliance posture almost no startup has.

3. **`platform/edge` (42 yaml) + `platform/observability` (22) + `platform/edge-runtime` (Rust) +
   `platform/messaging` (Go)** — real multi-language production systems, not scripts.

4. **The "survives losing one node" theme runs through 6 layers** (`llm`, `guacamole`,
   `healthchecks`, `otto-golden`, `weave-gitops`, `scheduling`). Single-node survivability was
   engineered deliberately and repeatedly, after the 2→1 node swap on 2026-08-27.

5. **`agent-workforce` has "a name and nothing else. No Role, no RoleBinding."** That is ADR 0025
   (agents hold no write on production) implemented as an *absence* — the most honest form of the
   control.

6. **`concierge`: "One object runs here: nothing."** Consent-gated by construction — the namespace
   exists as a fence before the workload does.

7. **`rule-coverage/ungraded.yaml`** is the estate's own list of rules with no gate. The estate
   publishes its own governance debt.
