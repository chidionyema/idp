## How a guard answers

A guard prints nothing and exits 0 to pass. It refuses with exit 2 and the reason on stderr, or with `{"decision": "block", "reason": ...}` on stdout. A guard that crashes or exceeds its budget is `NoVerdict`, and the door answers no (fail-closed). Every run is a row in the hook ledger (`hook-run.py`), which is where a claim that a guard ran is checked.
