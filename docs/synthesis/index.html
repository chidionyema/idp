The Factory Brief

Four artifacts, one thesis. The estate is a fractal factory — one kind of thing (a factory that takes an order and produces a result) recursing at every scale, where the result is itself a factory. The overlap is intentional: a research lab runs the bleeding edge beside the incumbent, and sheds the loser. This brief says what exists (the Inventory), why it must be shaped this way (the Programme), and how to machine-check that shape (the Contract).
