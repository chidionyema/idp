*`prm` · founder essay 2026-09-14, the Reasoning Gateway ("Process Reward Models... evaluates the correctness of each step... rather than just the final outcome") · planes: ci*

## The registry's own lines

- when it passes: every step of a transcript scores at least 0.8 on factual correctness, relevance and efficiency, so a hallucinated or off-goal step is caught at the step, not only when the transcript is finished
- when it refuses: a step made a completed-work claim (e.g. "I verified the database is up") with zero independent evidence gathered by that point in the transcript -- the blind-assumption case the founder's essay names. Deterministic: reuses bin/idp-epistemic's claim/evidence/witness logic and bin/idp-trajectory's plan-binding logic, imported rather than re-implemented (R43); no frontier LLM judge, per the ticket's own stated risk.
