PROOF 3 -- 'ran and failed' is a different state from 'never ran'
Captured 2026-08-24T02:29:00Z.

Second angle, independent of Healthchecks: launchctl reports each job's own
last exit status. Estate jobs only (com.apple.*, homebrew.*, com.valvesoftware.*
and com.ollama.* excluded).

$ launchctl list | awk "non-zero exit, estate labels only"
  exit=-15   ai.estate.consultd
  exit=-15   com.chidionyema.maestro
  exit=-15   com.founder.boardserve
  exit=1     ai.architect.gateway
  exit=1     com.estate.costsentinel
  exit=1     com.founder.board
  exit=1     com.founder.sciencecollect
  exit=1     com.prospector.estate-inventory
  exit=1     com.prospector.launchd-held
  exit=1     com.prospector.log-rotation

  10 estate jobs last exited non-zero. Each one RAN. None of them missed a schedule.

Negative exit codes are signals (-9 SIGKILL, -15 SIGTERM), which is a process
that was killed rather than one that failed on its own. Positive codes are the
job's own failure. hc-wrap.sh pings the exit code, so both land as a 'down'
check -- and a 'down' check therefore never means a missed schedule.
